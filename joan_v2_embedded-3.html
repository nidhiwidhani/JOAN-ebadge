ePaper Technology

A reusable, wireless, interactive e-paper badge for visitors, employees, and events. One piece of hardware, updated on demand for whichever job it's needed for.
VISITOR

Know who's on site, without a printout.

Checks in at the kiosk, wears a badge with name, company, photo and host, and drops it back in the dock afterward. It clears itself automatically.
EMPLOYEE

One badge, every day, no reprints.

Assigned in the Joan app and ready before day one. Updates re-sync instead of reprinting, and a lost badge is revoked immediately.
EVENT

Hundreds of badges, ready before doors open.

Written at registration or loaded in batches beforehand. A misspelled name or a substitute delegate is a re-sync, not a reprint.
